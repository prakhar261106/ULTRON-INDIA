ULTRON News Intelligence Engine

Purpose:
Analyse important Indian financial and corporate news.

Main responsibilities:
- Collect official NSE and BSE announcements.
- Analyse company announcements.
- Analyse financial news.
- Verify news authenticity.
- Detect duplicate news.
- Use FinBERT sentiment analysis.
- Calculate positive, negative and neutral probabilities.
- Analyse news materiality and impact.
- Detect potentially market-moving developments.
- Calculate India Market Fear and Greed Index.

Output:
Verified news intelligence, sentiment, impact assessment and market sentiment.

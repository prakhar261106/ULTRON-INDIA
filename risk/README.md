ULTRON Independent Risk Engine

Purpose:
Independently validate every proposed trade.

Main responsibilities:
- Entry price validation.
- Stop-loss calculation.
- Target calculation.
- Position sizing.
- Capital allocation.
- Risk per trade.
- Brokerage and transaction costs.
- Slippage estimation.
- Net reward-to-risk calculation.
- Maximum daily loss validation.
- Trade approval or rejection.

The Risk Engine can reject any signal from the Master Engine.

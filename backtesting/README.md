ULTRON Backtesting

Purpose:
Test ULTRON trading strategies using historical market data.

Main responsibilities:
- Historical signal simulation.
- Entry and exit simulation.
- Stop-loss and target testing.
- Brokerage and tax calculations.
- Slippage modelling.
- Net profit and loss.
- Maximum drawdown.
- Profit factor.
- Strategy comparison.

All backtests must avoid look-ahead and survivorship bias.
